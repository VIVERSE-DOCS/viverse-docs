# How to get your content curated

Curation is a manual review of indexed content by the VIVERSE team. This page explains what curation gives you, what reviewers look for, and how the review works.

***

## What VIVERSE curates

VIVERSE is trying to raise the standard of quality for games and experiences in the web browser. Curation highlights the best artistry, storytelling, and originality on the platform, along with engaging gameplay, so players can find work made with care.

Because of that, VIVERSE is unlikely to curate content that:

* Feels derivative, such as a clone of an existing game.
* Is too hypercasual to stand out from other browser games.
* Clearly feels mass-produced with AI, or uses AI-generated assets that copy other creators' work.

Curation isn't a score you can reach with a checklist. Meeting every technical criterion below makes your content eligible, but reviewers decide based on the experience as a whole.

## What curation gives you

Curated content is weighted into the algorithm that powers VIVERSE's curated pages and the sidebar on content detail pages, which suggests more content while players are in a game.

The algorithm also accounts for engagement and retention. That's separate from the curation review: reviewers evaluate the content itself, and player engagement affects how curated content is weighted afterward.

## What reviewers look for

Reviewers play your content on the reference devices and evaluate the areas below. Your content must already meet every [indexed requirement](indexed-requirements.md).

| Area | What reviewers look for |
| --- | --- |
| Optimization | An average of at least 45 FPS on the reference devices for each device type you declare, and fast loading |
| Tags | Your content matches the tags you set in VIVERSE Studio |
| Bugs and UX | Largely bug free, with clear controls, instructions, and error messages |
| Accessibility | Readable text, good contrast, and no unwarned flashing effects |
| Quality | Artistry, storytelling, originality, and engaging gameplay |

### Optimization

The curation bar is an average of **45 FPS**, compared with 24 FPS for indexing. Reviewers measure it on the same [reference devices](indexed-requirements.md#performance) used for indexed review. Reviewers also look for fast loading. Optimize compression, bundle size, texture resolution, and asset streaming so players reach gameplay quickly.

### Tags

Set accurate tags in VIVERSE Studio. Tags help players find content they'll enjoy, so content that doesn't match its tags isn't curated.

### Bugs and user experience

Curated content should be largely bug free. Reviewers expect no P0 or P1 issues, P2 issues to be rare, and only a few minor P3 issues. See the [severity levels](indexed-requirements.md#bugs) for definitions. Reviewers also look for:

* Clear labels and instructions for the main controls.
* Clear feedback when something goes wrong, such as a network error, with a way to recover.
* UI that works at every screen size and orientation you declare.

### Accessibility

Reviewers check that your content is usable by as many players as possible. They look for:

* Readable text and enough contrast between text and background.
* Keyboard navigation for essential menus where it applies.
* Captions or subtitles for essential spoken information.
* No flashing or strobing effects without a warning or a way to turn them off.

### VIVERSE sign-in and cloud save

Reviewers consider it a plus when your content uses the VIVERSE login and storage SDKs, so signed-in VIVERSE players keep their progress without creating another account. See [VIVERSE login](https://docs.viverse.com/developer-tools/login-and-authentication-for-the-sdk) and [VIVERSE cloud save](https://docs.viverse.com/developer-tools/storage-sdk) to add them.

## How curation reviews work

You don't need to request a curation review. The VIVERSE team reviews indexed content for curation, and a review usually takes 1 to 3 weeks.

Before then, play your indexed content on your declared devices and compare it with the criteria on this page, especially the 45 FPS optimization bar.

If you haven't heard from us and want to ask about your content, [open a support ticket](https://support.viverse.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=7051418762381). Include the name of your content and a link to it on VIVERSE.

## If your content isn't curated

Your content stays indexed and keeps appearing in basic discovery. To ask VIVERSE to reconsider, make meaningful changes, such as improved performance, new content, or a better first-time experience. Then [open a support ticket](https://support.viverse.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=7051418762381) and describe what changed.

## Staying curated

Curation isn't permanent. VIVERSE can remove curation at any time if new technical issues are measured. Over time, content may also leave curation if it no longer compares well to other content on the platform.

Every update goes through the [indexed review](indexed-requirements.md) before it goes live. If an update meaningfully changes your content, VIVERSE may reconsider its curation.
